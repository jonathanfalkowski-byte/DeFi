DeFi Protocol Evaluation

A structured checklist for researching a protocol. It is not a recommendation and cannot catch every risk. Answer Yes, No, or leave Unknown. Unknown is not the same as Yes: unanswered critical items cap the verdict.

Safety & Track Record
Protocol has been live for 2+ years Protocols that survive bear markets have proven resilience Weight: 3x (Critical: must be Yes)
Protocol has been audited by major firms Look for OpenZeppelin, Trail of Bits, ConsenSys, or Certora audits Weight: 3x (Critical: must be Yes)
No major hacks or exploits in past 2 years Check rekt.news and DefiLlama's hacks dashboard for incident history Weight: 3x (Critical: must be Yes)
Team has shipped products before Not their first rodeo; prior successful projects Weight: 2x (High)
Protocol has institutional backing Funded by reputable VCs. Weak signal on its own: well-funded projects have still failed Weight: 1x (Medium)
Economics & Sustainability
APY is between 2-20% (not >100%) High yields signal unsustainable incentive structure Weight: 3x (Critical: must be Yes)
Protocol profits from transaction fees (not just token emissions) Protocol should generate revenue independently Weight: 2x (High)
TVL (Total Value Locked) has been stable or growing Declining TVL = users leaving = red flag Weight: 2x (High)
Token emissions are decreasing or have ended Infinite token printing = price collapse (eventually) Weight: 2x (High)
Protocol is profitable (revenue > token emissions) Sustainable economics = long-term survival Weight: 1x (Medium)
Risk Assessment
Smart contract code is well-tested and public Code should be open source on GitHub; ask for audit reports Weight: 3x (Critical: must be Yes)
Protocol has insurance or coverage for hacks Nexus Mutual cover, a protocol safety module, or an in-house insurance fund Weight: 2x (High)
No excessive leverage or flashloan attack vectors Liquidation cascade risk should be minimal Weight: 2x (High)
Team is doxxed (publicly known identities) Anonymous teams = higher scam risk Weight: 1x (Medium)
Protocol governance is decentralized (or on roadmap) Avoid protocols where one person controls everything Weight: 1x (Medium)
Market Position
Protocol is #1 or #2 in its category By TVL and usage in its category, e.g. lending, stablecoin swaps, liquid staking Weight: 2x (High)
Protocol token is trading on major exchanges Listing on large exchanges is a mild legitimacy signal, not proof of safety Weight: 1x (Medium)
Community engagement is active (not hype-driven) Discord/Twitter should focus on tech, not price speculation Weight: 1x (Medium)
